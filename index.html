Delete Your Data

App: HiddenCalculator | Developer: Ammad Saleem

You can request deletion of your account and personal data linked to HiddenCalculator at any time. This page explains how to submit a data deletion request.

If you signed in with Google, Facebook or email

  1. Send an email to ammadsaleem252@gmail.com with the subject Delete my data.
  2. Include the email address associated with your account so we can identify it. Please send the request from the same email address if possible.
  3. We will contact you to confirm your request and process eligible data deletion requests within 30 days.
Data that may be deleted, where applicable:
  • Your account from Firebase Authentication
  • Personal data associated with your account
  • Cloud backup files and related stored information, if your app stores them

If you used guest mode

If guest-mode data is stored only on your device, you can delete it by going to:

Settings → Apps → HiddenCalculator → Storage → Clear data

Uninstalling the app may also remove locally stored app data.

Remove app access from Facebook or Google (optional)

  • Facebook: Settings & privacy → Settings → Apps and Websites → select HiddenCalculator → Remove.
  • Google: Google Account Permissions → select HiddenCalculator → Remove access.

Removing app access does not necessarily delete data already stored by the app. To request deletion, contact us using the email above.
