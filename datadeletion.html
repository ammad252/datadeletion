You can ask us to delete your account and all data linked to it at any time. This page explains how.

If you signed in with Google, Facebook or email

  1. Send an email to [YOUR EMAIL] with the subject "Delete my data".
  2. Write the email address of your account (or the name on your Facebook account) so we can find it. Please send it from the same email address if you can.
  3. We will confirm by email and delete your data within 30 days.
What we delete:
  • Your account (sign-in record) from Firebase Authentication
  • Your cloud backup: encrypted vault files, file names, folder names
  • Your backup key and the stored PIN hash

If you used guest mode

Guest data is stored only on your phone, and we do not have a copy. To delete it, uninstall the app or go to Settings → Apps → [YOUR APP NAME] → Storage → Clear data.

Remove the app's access from your Facebook or Google account (optional)

  • Facebook: Settings & privacy → Settings → Apps and Websites → select [YOUR APP NAME] → Remove.
  • Google: open myaccount.google.com/permissions and remove [YOUR APP NAME].

Removing access this way stops future sign-in, but it does not delete your backup. To delete the backup, email us as described above.
