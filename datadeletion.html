Delete Your Data

App: HiddenCalculator | Developer: Ammad Saleem

You can request deletion of your account and personal data linked to HiddenCalculator at any time. This page explains how to submit a deletion request.

1. If You Signed In With Google, Facebook or Email

  1. Send an email to: ammadsaleem252@gmail.com
  2. Use the subject: Delete My Data
  3. Include the email address associated with your account so we can identify your account.
  4. We will review your request and process eligible account and data deletion requests within 30 days.
Data that may be deleted, where stored by the app:
  • Firebase Authentication account information.
  • Encrypted cloud backup files.
  • Vault file names and folder information.
  • Stored backup keys and PIN hashes.

2. If You Used Guest Mode

Guest data is stored locally on your device. If no cloud backup was created, we do not have a copy of your guest files.

To remove guest data, you can uninstall the app or follow these steps:

Settings → Apps → HiddenCalculator → Storage → Clear Data

Please note that clearing app data or uninstalling the app may permanently remove locally stored files.

3. Remove App Access From Facebook or Google

You can also remove HiddenCalculator's access from your social accounts.

  • Facebook: Go to Facebook Settings → Apps and Websites, select HiddenCalculator and choose Remove.
  • Google: Visit Google Account Permissions and remove HiddenCalculator if listed.

Removing app access does not automatically delete any existing account or cloud backup. Submit an email deletion request for that purpose.
